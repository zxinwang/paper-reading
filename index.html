Explain the mechanism讲清机制

First-use terminology, worked examples, method pipelines and important derivations, adapted to the actual paper.专业术语首次解释，结合具体例子、方法流程与关键推导，按论文实际内容展开。

Keep evidence visible让证据可追溯

Separate paper claims, prior work, inference and hypothesis. Check numbers and disclose missing access.区分论文原文、前序研究、合理推断与未验证假设；复核数字，说明缺失材料。

A feasible seven-day replication, controlled counterexamples and a candidate research idea with risks and baselines.给出资源可行的七天最小复现、受控反例，以及有基线和失败条件的候选研究构想。
